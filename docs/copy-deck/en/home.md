## 04 — Why continuous monitoring (dark instrument section, interactive diagram)

**Eyebrow:** 03 — WHY CONTINUOUS

**Headline:** A test records one moment. A fault does not wait for the next one.

**Body:**
Most insulation and network faults do not appear suddenly. They develop over weeks or months, and for most of that time the asset keeps operating normally. A permanently installed monitor measures while the asset is in service, records the trend and raises an alarm when a set limit is passed. Maintenance can then be planned around the condition of the asset instead of a fixed calendar or an unplanned outage.

### Interactive diagram: "Periodic test vs continuous"

**Diagram title (mono):** `FIG. 1 — ONE ASSET, TWO WAYS OF LOOKING AT IT (ILLUSTRATIVE)`

**Toggle labels:** Periodic test · Continuous monitoring

**Axis labels:** `CONDITION INDICATOR` (Y) · `TIME` (X) · `ALARM LIMIT` (threshold)

**Step captions — Periodic test view**
1. **Test 1.** The asset is tested. The result is good. This is one point on the timeline.
2. **The week after.** A defect starts. Nothing shows it. The asset keeps running normally.
3. **Between tests.** The defect grows. The last test report still says "good", and it is still correct about the day it was written.
4. **Test 2, or not.** If the fault is slower than the test interval, the next test finds it. If it is faster, the outage finds it first.

**Step captions — Continuous view**
1. **Baseline.** The monitor measures while the asset is in service. A normal level is established for this asset.
2. **Change.** The defect starts. The trend leaves its baseline. The change is small, but it is on record.
3. **Alarm.** The trend passes a set limit. The asset is still in service.
4. **Decision.** There is time to confirm the finding, plan the outage and order what is needed.

**Readout chips (mono):**
- Periodic: `LAST KNOWN STATE: GOOD` → `ACTUAL STATE: UNKNOWN`
- Continuous: `TREND STABLE` → `TREND RISING` → `ALARM LIMIT PASSED`

**Caption under diagram (small):**
Illustrative. Real faults develop at different speeds, and no monitor sees every failure mode. The point is the difference between a point and a line.

**Honesty line (pull-quote size):**
Periodic testing is not wrong. It is a photograph. Monitoring is the film.

| # | Name | Blurb | Tells you | Link label |
