## 04 — How it works: Define → Build → Run

**Eyebrow:** 03 — HOW IT WORKS

**Headline:** Define. Build. Run.

**Intro:** Three steps. At each one you bring something and you get something.

### 01 — Define
**One line:** Agree what is being asked for.
- **You bring:** Item list, technical specification, applicable standards, target delivery.
- **You get:** A clear scope and a short list of open questions.

### 02 — Build
**One line:** Turn the scope into a proposal you can compare.
- **You bring:** Answers to the open questions; your priorities on specification, quality and delivery time.
- **You get:** A comparable proposal from matched manufacturers. The choice stays with you.

### 03 — Run
**One line:** Follow the order to delivery.
- **You bring:** Your selection and order.
- **You get:** Order, production follow-up, documents and logistics through one contact; a status you can follow.

**Under the steps:** The scope of each engagement is set out in the agreement.

**What "comparable" means (short explainer):**
Offers from different manufacturers rarely arrive in the same form. One quotes a different standard, another a different delivery term. A comparable proposal sets them against the same specification, line by line, so the differences are visible before you choose.

## 08 — FAQ

**Q: Is Glinker Group a manufacturer?**
No. Glinker Group matches your requirement with Turkish manufacturers and manages the process through one contact.

**Q: Who chooses the manufacturer?**
You do. You receive a comparable proposal from matched manufacturers. The choice stays with you.

**Q: What do you need from me to start?**
An item list, the technical specification, the applicable standards and your target delivery. If some of that is missing, send what you have; the open questions come back to you in the Define step.

**Q: I only need an alternative for one item. Is that possible?**
Yes. A preliminary study can be started for items where you are looking for an alternative.

**Q: Do you work with buyers outside Türkiye?**
Yes. Glinker Group acts as a Trade Facilitator: one point of access between international buyers and Turkish manufacturers.

**Q: What exactly is included in an engagement?**
The scope of each engagement is set out in the agreement. In general it follows the three steps: Define, Build, Run.

**Q: My need is not on the scope list.**
Send it. You can also contact us for needs in engineering and project design, building systems, software and cyber security.
