Why continuous monitoring?
Most insulation and network faults do not appear suddenly. They develop over weeks or months, and for most of that time the asset keeps operating normally. A periodic test records one moment. A permanently installed monitor measures while the asset is in service, records the trend and raises an alarm when a set limit is passed, so maintenance can be planned around the condition of the asset.
Generation, transmission, rail, defence-industry and other critical facilities use the same assets. For these, and for other solutions and field test instruments, contact us.
