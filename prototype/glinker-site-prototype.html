Online Monitoring

Faults develop over weeks. Continuous monitoring shows the trend while the asset stays in service.

Select an asset

Technologies

Why continuous monitoring?

Most insulation and network faults do not appear suddenly. They develop over weeks or months, and for most of that time the asset keeps operating normally. A periodic test records one moment. A permanently installed monitor measures while the asset is in service, records the trend and raises an alarm when a set limit is passed, so maintenance can be planned around the condition of the asset.

Generation, transmission, rail, defence-industry and other critical facilities use the same assets. For these, and for other solutions and field test instruments, contact us.

Online Monitoring / Partial Discharge Monitoring

Partial Discharge Monitoring

Shows whether an insulation defect is stable or growing, while the asset stays energised.

What it detects

  • Voids and cracksInside solid insulation.
  • Surface trackingOn bushings and terminations.
  • Stator dischargesSlot and end-winding discharges in stator windings.
  • Rising trendChanges in magnitude and repetition rate over time.

A single measurement shows discharge is present. A trend tells you whether to act.

Where it applies

  • Power transformer
  • Generator & HV motor
  • Cable terminations

Online Monitoring / DGA Monitoring

Dissolved Gas Analysis (DGA) Monitoring

Online gas measurement gives an early warning between laboratory oil samples.

What it detects

  • H₂HydrogenProduced by most developing faults: a general early indicator.
  • C₂H₂AcetyleneAssociated with arcing; even small amounts need attention.
  • MoistureReduces dielectric strength and speeds up paper ageing.

A two-gas monitor shows that something is developing. Fault type needs laboratory DGA.

Where it applies

  • Oil-filled power transformer

Product

InsuLogix G2

Megger's online monitor for transformer oil.

Per manufacturer data
Measures
Hydrogen, acetylene, moisture in oil
Detection limits
25 ppm H₂ · 0.5 ppm C₂H₂ · 2 ppm moisture
Mounting

Weight
Approx. 13.5 kg
Communication
Modbus RTU / TCP; DNP3 and IEC 61850 optional

Two-gas monitor. It does not replace laboratory DGA; ratio methods do not apply to its readings alone.

Standards

Guide for interpreting full multi-gas DGA results from laboratory analysis.

Guide for interpreting gases generated in oil-immersed transformers.
