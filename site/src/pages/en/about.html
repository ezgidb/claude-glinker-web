04How we work

Three principles.

  1. 01

    One contact

    You deal with one contact. In supply, that contact carries the order, production follow-up, documents and logistics. In monitoring, the same contact carries the technical conversation. The branching happens on our side, not yours.

  2. 02

    The choice stays with you

    We are not a manufacturer and we are not tied to one. You receive a comparable proposal from matched manufacturers and you choose. In monitoring, the asset owner sets the limits and makes the decision; the measurement supports it.

  3. 03

    We say what a measurement cannot tell you

    Every method has a limit. A two-gas monitor is not a full dissolved gas analysis. A trend is not a failure date. Asset management software adds no new measurement. We state these limits on the page, next to the product, because a specification built on a misunderstanding costs more than a plain answer.
