03Why continuous

A test records one moment. A fault does not wait for the next one.

Most insulation and network faults do not appear suddenly. They develop over weeks or months, and for most of that time the asset keeps operating normally.

A permanently installed monitor measures while the asset is in service, records the trend and raises an alarm when a set limit is passed. Maintenance can then be planned around the condition of the asset instead of a fixed calendar or an unplanned outage.

Periodic testing is not wrong. It is a photograph. Monitoring is the film.

04The power chain

Follow the line. Every asset fails in its own way.

From the grid connection to the last distribution board, power passes through a chain of assets. What they share: faults usually develop over weeks or months. What differs is where to look and what to measure.

05Technologies

Five ways to see what is developing.

Each method answers one question. None answers all of them. Each technology page says what the measurement can and cannot tell you.

T1

Partial Discharge Monitoring

Small discharges inside insulation are both a symptom and a cause of damage. Monitoring them online shows whether a defect is stable or growing.

Tells youIs the insulation defect growing?

{{> arrow}} T2

Dissolved Gas Analysis (DGA) Monitoring

A developing fault in an oil-filled transformer releases gases into the oil. Online hydrogen, acetylene and moisture monitoring gives an early warning between laboratory samples.

Tells youIs something developing in the oil, and how fast?

{{> arrow}} T3

Cable & MV Monitoring

A cable or feeder is a route, not a single item. Watch the known weak points on HV cables. Locate faults quickly on MV overhead feeders.

Tells youWhere along the route?

{{> arrow}} T4

Distribution Transformer & Power Quality Monitoring

A compact in-oil sensor for the many small transformers, and a Class A analyser for the quality of the supply they deliver.

Tells youWhat condition is the oil in? What quality is the voltage?

{{> arrow}} T5

Asset Management

Asset performance management software that brings test and monitoring data together, so maintenance and investment go to the right assets first. It adds no new measurement.

Tells youWhich asset first?
