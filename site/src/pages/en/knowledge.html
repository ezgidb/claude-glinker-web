  1. Digest · Megger Knowledge Hub · 7 October 2025

    Choosing a partial discharge monitoring system

    • Partial discharge
    • Power transformer
    • Generator & motor
    What it says
    Partial discharge systems fall into distinct categories, with permanent online monitoring at one end and portable or periodic measurement at the other; the choice follows from the asset type, operating requirements and the purpose of the monitoring.
    Why it matters
    The decision is about the asset and its criticality first and the instrument second, which is a useful order for a specification.
    Who it is for
    Maintenance and asset management teams preparing a monitoring specification.

  2. Digest · Megger · June 2026

    Why hydrogen and acetylene are the two gases to watch

    • Dissolved gas analysis
    • Power transformer
    What it says
    Hydrogen appears early in most developing transformer faults, while acetylene points to high-energy faults; following both online gives a warning between laboratory samples.
    Why it matters
    It explains what a two-gas online monitor is for, and by implication what still needs a full laboratory analysis.
    Who it is for
    Transformer engineers and substation maintenance managers.
