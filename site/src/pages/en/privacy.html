Privacy Notice (KVKK)

This notice explains how Glinker Group processes personal data sent through the contact form on this website. It is given under Article 10 of Turkish Law No. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data (“KVKK”).

← Back to Contact

In short

  • We use what you send only to respond to your request.
  • The contact form does not store your data on this website. It prepares an e-mail that you send from your own e-mail account.
  • We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.
  • You have rights under Article 11 of the KVKK and can use them by writing to koordinasyon@glinkergroup.com.

3.Why do we process your data?

We process your personal data only for the following purposes:

  • to read and respond to your request;
  • to arrange a technical meeting or prepare a proposal, where you ask for one;
  • to keep a record of our correspondence with you, to the extent needed to follow up your request and to meet our legal obligations.

We do not use your data for marketing communications unless you separately and explicitly agree to that.

4.On what legal basis, and how, is your data collected?

Your data is collected by electronic means, through the e-mail you send to us after using the contact form, or through an e-mail you write to us directly.

We process it on the following legal bases in Article 5 of the KVKK:

  • processing is necessary for the establishment or performance of a contract, where your request concerns a proposal or an order (Article 5/2-c);
  • processing is necessary for our legitimate interests in responding to enquiries addressed to us, provided that this does not harm your fundamental rights and freedoms (Article 5/2-f);
  • processing is necessary to comply with a legal obligation, where one applies (Article 5/2-ç).

6.How long do we keep your data?

We keep your personal data for as long as needed to respond to your request and to follow up the correspondence, and afterwards for any period that the applicable legislation requires. When these periods end, the data is deleted, destroyed or anonymised.

7.What are your rights?

Under Article 11 of the KVKK, you have the right to apply to us and to:

  1. learn whether your personal data is processed;
  2. request information about the processing, if it has been processed;
  3. learn the purpose of the processing and whether the data is used in line with that purpose;
  4. know the third parties to whom your data is transferred, in Türkiye or abroad;
  5. request correction of your data if it is incomplete or inaccurate;
  6. request deletion or destruction of your data under the conditions set out in Article 7 of the KVKK;
  7. request that third parties to whom your data has been transferred be notified of such correction, deletion or destruction;
  8. object to a result against you that arises from analysis of your data exclusively by automated systems;
  9. claim compensation if you suffer damage because of unlawful processing of your data.

8.How can you use your rights?

Write to koordinasyon@glinkergroup.com. Please state your full name, the right you wish to use and the details of your request, and write from the e-mail address you used to contact us, so that we can verify that the request is yours. We may ask for further information to confirm your identity.

We will conclude your request free of charge as soon as possible and at the latest within thirty days, as required by Article 13 of the KVKK. If the request requires a separate cost, a fee may be charged according to the tariff set by the Personal Data Protection Board.

If your application is refused, if you find our answer insufficient, or if we do not answer in time, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Personal Data Protection Board (Kişisel Verileri Koruma Kurulu) within the periods set out in Article 14 of the KVKK.
